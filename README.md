# Customer Data Analysis | Excel, MySQL, Power BI

## Overview
This project studies customer shopping behaviour across multiple malls. The goal is to understand who buys, what they buy, how they pay and which malls perform best.

## Dataset
Customer shopping dataset with details such as gender, age, category, quantity, price, payment method, shopping mall and invoice date.

## Tools Used
- MySQL: data querying and analysis
- Excel: pivot tables and charts
- Power BI: interactive dashboard

## What I Did
1. Explored the dataset and checked it for errors.
2. Wrote MySQL queries to find sales by gender, age group, category, payment method and mall.
3. Built pivot tables and charts in Excel to compare the results.
4. Created an interactive Power BI dashboard to show the findings clearly.

## Key Findings
- Women make up about 60% of shoppers, units sold and revenue.
- Clothing, Shoes and Technology together give most of the revenue.
- The category mix is almost the same across all age groups.
- Shoppers aged 18 to 65 are spread almost evenly. Shoppers above 65 contribute less.
- Credit and debit cards together are used more than cash.
- A few malls, such as Kanyon, Mall of Istanbul and Metrocity, sell much more than smaller malls.
- Clothing has many peaks in the year. Technology and Shoes rise together from June to September. Toys rise in the last two quarters.
